3D Medial Axis Approximation

May 2026 – Aug 2026 · Computational Geometry
C++17 CUDA Voronoi Diagrams

A C++17 tool that extracts an approximate medial-axis sheet complex from a closed triangle mesh. It builds a 3D Delaunay tetrahedralization of surface samples with robust predicates, keeps the interior Voronoi polygons supported by inward poles, and assembles them into sheets with seams and junctions. On the Fertility model it recovers 30,146 sheet triangles from 11,602 Voronoi polygons.

Delaunay Triangulation

Apr 2026 · CS 274 Computational Geometry
Computational Geometry Python Algorithms

Incremental-insertion Delaunay triangulation built on Guibas–Stolfi quad-edges, comparing two point-location strategies: a walking search and a History DAG. With randomized insertion, the History DAG triangulates 1,000,000 points in about 11 minutes, while the walking method doesn't finish within 30, and it cuts orientation-test calls by 94%.
